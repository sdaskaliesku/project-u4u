16 August 2023 was the cut-off date for Ukrainians to apply for TPS. Those who arrived before this date remain TPS-eligible. Those who arrived after this date have begun losing their legal status as of 16 August 2025, since DHS has not been processing re-parole applications.

This is happening while Ukraine is still under full-scale war. Many parolees cannot return safely, yet have no available pathway to extend their lawful status in the U.S.
